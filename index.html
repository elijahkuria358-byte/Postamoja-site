PostaMoja

PostaMoja is a personal automation tool built by Mumchii Production to schedule and auto-post video content to social media platforms on behalf of its own account holder.

Privacy Policy

PostaMoja only accesses the social media accounts explicitly connected by its owner for the sole purpose of uploading scheduled video content. No data is collected from or shared with any third party. No personal data belonging to end users, other than the account owner's own credentials required for posting, is stored or processed.

Terms of Service

PostaMoja is used solely by its owner (Mumchii Production / Elijah Ngugi) to automate the posting of their own original video content to their own social media accounts. It is not offered as a public service to third parties at this time.
